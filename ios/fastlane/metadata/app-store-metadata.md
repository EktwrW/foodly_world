### Description - English

Foodly is the easiest way to discover restaurants, browse digital menus, and make reservations — all from your phone.

Whether you're exploring a new city or looking for the best spot around the corner, Foodly helps you find exactly what you're craving.

FOR DINERS:
- Discover restaurants, bars, cafes, and food trucks near you
- Browse beautiful digital menus with photos and prices
- Make reservations instantly — no phone calls needed
- Save your favorite spots and share them with friends
- Search by cuisine, dish name, or location
- Voice search for hands-free discovery

FOR RESTAURANT OWNERS:
- Create a stunning digital menu in minutes
- Reach new customers who are searching nearby
- Manage reservations effortlessly
- Promote your business with photos, hours, and special offers
- Get discovered by tourists and locals alike

Foodly is available in English, Spanish, and Portuguese. Currently serving restaurants in Portugal, Argentina, and Venezuela — with more countries coming soon.
