Foodly is the all-in-one platform for local food businesses — and the people who love them.

FOR YOUR BUSINESS — FREE
Restaurant, bar, café, food truck, catering company or private chef? Build your digital presence in minutes and start reaching new customers today. No setup fees, no subscriptions.

- Create a beautiful digital menu in minutes — import it straight from a photo and share it with a QR code
- Get discovered by customers searching nearby
- Manage reservations from one simple dashboard
- Receive service requests and send quotes — built for caterers and private chefs
- Publish promotions and updates to boost your visibility
- Track real-time analytics on views, reservations and performance
- AI-powered tools that do the heavy lifting for you

Built for every kind of food business: restaurants, bars, cafés, food trucks, catering companies and private chefs.

FOR FOOD LOVERS
Discover the best places to eat around you, browse rich digital menus, reserve a table in seconds, follow your favorites and join Buzz — Foodly's own social network, where you see what local diners are loving right now and share what you find to WhatsApp and social media in one tap.

FREE TO USE
Foodly is free for businesses and diners alike. No subscriptions, no hidden fees. Businesses pay a 3% commission only on orders paid through the app, and whoever pays adds a €0.25 platform fee per transaction, always shown before confirming. Pay at the counter and neither applies.

AVAILABLE NOW — ORDER FROM YOUR TABLE, NO WAITING
Scan the table's QR code and order with whoever is with you: everyone adds what they want to the same order, with no passing the phone around and no waiting for someone to come take it. The bill splits itself — by item or evenly — and everyone sees exactly what they owe before heading to the counter. On the other side, the business watches the order arrive in real time and always knows which table it came from.

Businesses that turn on payments can also charge inside the app, by card or local payment method.

Available now in Portugal, Spain, Argentina and Venezuela, with more markets coming soon.
