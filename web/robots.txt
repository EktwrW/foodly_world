# menu.foodly.solutions — public business menus, served as a Flutter Web app.
#
# Each business uuid resolves to a public menu page (the same surface that
# the QR codes and "share menu" links produce). These pages are intentionally
# crawlable: the more they index, the more local-search visibility each
# Foodly customer gets ("pizzeria covilhã menu", etc.).
#
# Disallow `/api/`, `/auth/`, and the Flutter engine assets to keep the
# crawl budget on actual menu URLs. The Flutter Web SPA ships its routes
# under `/<businessUuid>` paths via in-app routing, so there's nothing
# to disallow at the static-files level beyond engine internals.

User-agent: *
Allow: /
Disallow: /flutter_service_worker.js
Disallow: /version.json
Disallow: /canvaskit/
Disallow: /assets/
